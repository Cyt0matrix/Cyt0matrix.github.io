Project 3: Incident Investigation & Triage Playbook

Category: Incident Response & SOC Operations

Objective

Bridge the gap between detection alerts and operational response by authoring a formal Security Operations Center (SOC) Incident Report (INC-2026-001) for host discovery activities.

Incident Overview (INC-2026-001)

  • Severity: Medium
  • Detection Source: Splunk SIEM Alert (Suspicious Host Enumeration Activity)
  • Affected Asset: Windows 10 Endpoint (`192.168.0.3`)
  • Detection Vector: Sysmon Event ID 1 (Process Creation) matching reconnaissance binaries.

Analyst Triage & Investigation Steps

  • Step 1 (Alert Validation): Reviewed triggered alert data indicating rapid sequence execution of host discovery binaries.
  • Step 2 (Telemetry Extraction): Utilized regex SPL parsing to verify exact command-line arguments and user execution context.
  • Step 3 (Context & Scope): Analyzed parent processes and user privileges to evaluate threat severity.

Conclusion & Remediation

Classified as a Controlled Simulation (True Positive - Authorized Testing). Confirmed detection efficacy without generating false positive fatigue.
